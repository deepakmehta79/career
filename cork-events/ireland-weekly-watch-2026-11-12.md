As Presidency, Ireland is driving the Commission's overhaul of the tobacco excise directive, despite continued opposition from several member states.
[Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/politics/arid-41935416.html)

**Background (not this week) — US tariff exposure**
The US 15% baseline tariff on EU goods remains in place. Pharma and chips, Ireland's two largest US exports, have been exempt. CSO data show goods exports to the US fell 58% in Jan–Aug 2026, and total exports fell 24% to €138.4bn.
[Irish Examiner — exports Aug 2026](https://www.irishexaminer.com/business/economy/arid-41925007.html) · [IrishCentral](https://www.irishcentral.com/news/ireland-eu-us-tariff)

### Supply Chain

**2 Nov — InterTrade Ireland opens SupplyChain+ Pharma Cohort**
A cross-border programme that connects Irish SMEs with buyers from Almac, Sanofi and another global pharma company. It covers areas including temperature-controlled logistics. Spaces are limited and applications are closing soon.
[Londonderry Chamber](https://londonderrychamber.co.uk/intertrade-irelands-supplychain-pharma-cohort/)

### Artificial Intelligence

**13 Nov (tomorrow) — EU leaders' first AI-dedicated strategic discussion in Dublin**
The informal European Council is expected to focus on AI competitiveness and sovereignty, following on from the October Dublin Declaration.
[Dublin AI Declaration overview](https://shattered.io/dublin-ai-declaration-seven-priorities-epc-leaders-europe-2026/) · [Irish Presidency](https://irish-presidency.consilium.europa.eu/en/events/informal-meeting-of-eu-heads-of-state-or-government/)

**30 Oct–3 Nov — Ireland signs the IPCEI-AI political declaration**
Ireland joined 18 other member states backing an Important Project of Common European Interest (IPCEI) in AI. IPCEIs allow state-aid-backed joint investment in next-generation AI.
[fundsforNGOs News](https://news.fundsforngos.org/2026/11/03/ireland-backs-eu-ai-ipcei-ai-political-declaration/)

**2 Nov — "Ireland's AI moment" (Irish Times analysis)**
Microsoft data put Ireland's AI user share at 49.9%, the highest in the EU. Pitchbook records €256m across 75 Irish AI deals so far in 2026. The piece argues Ireland must move from adopting AI to building it, with the €121.7m Rinn AI national hub central to that.
[Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/11/02/irelands-ai-moment-making-the-leap-from-adopting-ai-to-building-it/) · [Tech Insider summary](https://tech-insider.org/ie/ireland-ai-adopting-to-building-rinn-ai-2026/)

**2 Nov — Central Bank of Ireland sets out AI and tokenisation priorities**
At a Dublin financial-system conference, the Central Bank set out its supervisory priorities on AI and tokenisation.
[EuropaWire](https://news.europawire.eu/central-bank-of-ireland-details-ai-and-tokenisation-priorities-at-dublin-financial-system-conference/eu-press-release/2026/11/02/14/27/04/185574/)

**Background — AI Act enforcement in Ireland**
Ireland uses a distributed model with 15 competent authorities, coordinated by a new AI Office of Ireland that the government said would be set up by Aug 2026. No update this week was found confirming its operational status.
