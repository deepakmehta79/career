### Geopolitics

**Budget 2027 delivered (6 October).** Simon Harris and Jack Chambers announced an €8.65bn package including €1.65bn of tax measures (higher-rate band widened by €2,500, CGT cut from 33% to 31%, minimum wage to €14.94) and R&D credit and Knowledge Development Box enhancements. The Fiscal Advisory Council warned of a "worse trajectory" for public finances and growing reliance on corporation tax.

### Supply Chain

**Section 232 pharma tariffs now in force for all firms.** The general effective date (29 September) has passed; EU-origin pharma is capped at 15%, with 0% for firms holding MFN-pricing and onshoring agreements (e.g. Pfizer, AstraZeneca, Lilly) and generics currently exempt. Irish exposure remains high given pharma's share of US-bound exports. I found no post-29 September reporting on changes.

**Data-centre grid constraints persist.** The regulator plans a gas-tariff discount for new data centres that accept supply interruptions; EirGrid forecasts data centres rising from about 22% of demand in 2026 to 31% by 2034, with no remaining Greater Dublin capacity for large connections.
