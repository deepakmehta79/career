### Supply Chain

**EU packaging regulation's first obligations bite from 12 August — no Irish transposition needed.** The PPWR's first tranche took effect on 12 August, applying directly to every business placing packaging on the Irish market. From that date manufacturers must hold Annex VII technical documentation and issue Declarations of Conformity per packaging type, evidencing compliance with the PFAS ban in food-contact packaging and heavy-metals limits, with records retained five years (ten for reusable formats). It lands hardest on Ireland's food and drink exporters.
[Accountancy Ireland](https://www.accountancyireland.ie/2026/07/03/irish-businesses-on-the-clock-as-new-eu-packaging-rules-tighten/content.html)

**Irish manufacturing keeps expanding, but export orders are the soft spot.** The AIB Ireland Manufacturing PMI came in at 55.1 in July, its second-highest reading since May 2022, with employment growing at the fastest rate in over four years. The caveat matters for supply chain planning: export order growth slowed to its weakest pace since February, and firms continued to report transport delays and raw-material availability constraints tied to wider geopolitical disruption.
[RTÉ](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/0804/1586341-aib-manufacturing-pmi/)

**Pharma tariff exposure stays the dominant variable in Ireland's trade outlook.** With medical and pharmaceutical products at €138.6bn — 53.2% of total goods exports in 2025 — and the US taking 42.9% of Irish exports, the phased Section 232 pharma regime continues to set the risk profile. Monthly figures remain distorted by the 2025 frontloading unwind, leaving underlying demand hard to read; Enterprise Ireland's tariff-response grants remain open to affected client companies.
[CSO via RTÉ](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/0217/1558929-cso-export-and-import-figures/) · [Enterprise Ireland](https://www.enterprise-ireland.com/en/news/enterprise-ireland-launches-new-grants-for-exporters-impacted-by-us-tariffs)

It arrives in step with the obligation — live since 2 August — that companies disclose when users are interacting with an AI system and when they are exposed to deepfakes or AI-generated content. The Regulation of Artificial Intelligence Bill 2026 supplies the domestic enforcement architecture.
