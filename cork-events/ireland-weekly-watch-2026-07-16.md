- **Ireland holds the EU Council Presidency** (since 1 July, through Dec 2026): priorities are competitiveness, security, digital/AI, and the 2028–34 EU budget talks. This is why Dublin is hosting a wave of informal EU ministerial meetings this month (Justice & Home Affairs 16–17 July, Research & Innovation 20–21 July, Environment & Climate 23–24 July) — closed-door, not open events, but useful context for why Dublin is busy with delegations right now. [Programme](https://irish-presidency.consilium.europa.eu/en/programme/programme-of-the-irish-presidency/)
- **Intel confirmed a €5bn ($5.7bn) AI-driven investment** in its Leixlip campus (13 July) — hundreds of high-tech roles, part of its Intel 3/Xeon 6 buildout. [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/07/13/intel-to-invest-5bn-in-leixlip-campus/)
- **AI Office of Ireland goes live 1 August** — the statutory body enforcing the EU AI Act domestically, with power from day one over prohibited-practice and AI-literacy obligations. [Acuity AI](https://acuityai.co/blog/ai-office-of-ireland-1-august-2026)
- **Two trade deadlines to watch:** the US Section 122 tariff is set to expire ~24 July (paving the way for Section 301 tariffs), and the US administration's threatened 100% tariff on branded pharma from firms without an "onshoring" deal lands 31 July — material for Ireland given ~€33bn/yr in pharma exports to the US. [PwC](https://www.pwc.ie/services/tax/insights/us-tariffs-15062026.html) · [pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/pharma-tariffs-set-arrive-us-eu-trade-deal)
- **Data centres are straining the grid** amid the current heatwave — now ~a third of national electricity demand, per EirGrid. [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/ireland/2026/07/14/data-centres-in-a-heatwave-heaping-demand-on-stretched-electric-grid-during-climate-crisis/)
